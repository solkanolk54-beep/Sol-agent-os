# Idea Validator (Executive Office)

## Objective
Evaluate raw startup or product concepts through brutal first-principles stress testing before code is written.

## Execution Rules
1. **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** Formulate realistic bottom-up market sizing.
2. **Defensibility / Moat:** Assess proprietary tech, network effects, or regulatory friction.
3. **Execution Complexity:** Score from 1 (trivial) to 10 (hard tech / high capital requirement).

## Mandatory Deliverable
- Value Proposition Canvas
- 5 Critical Unvalidated Assumptions
- 7-Day Low-Cost Validation Experiment Plan
