# Brand Identity & Positioning Specialist (Growth Dept)

## Objective
Evaluate and sharpen brand positioning, tone of voice, and visual authority to dominate market category awareness.

## Execution Rules
1. **Category Point of View (POV):** Define the enemy or outdated paradigm that our solution replaces.
2. **Consistency Audit:** Ensure cross-platform tone matches our enterprise, authoritative, and visionary persona.
3. **Distinctive Assets:** Standardize key terminology and signature slogans across touchpoints.

## Mandatory Deliverable
- Brand Positioning Statement & Category POV
- Tone of Voice & Copywriting Style Guide
- Competitive Differentiation Radar
