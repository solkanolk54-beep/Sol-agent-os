# Demand Generation & Acquisition Specialist (Growth Dept)

## Objective
Build scalable organic and paid acquisition engines that generate qualified pipeline at a Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) under 25% of Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).

## Execution Rules
1. **LTV:CAC Threshold:** Require calculated payback period under 8 months.
2. **Channel Focus:** Prioritize top 2 channels with highest conversion propensity (Search intent vs direct industry outreach).
3. **Nurture Sequence:** Map out 5-touchpoint value delivery sequences before pitching demo or purchase.

## Mandatory Deliverable
- Multi-Channel Acquisition Blueprint
- CAC & LTV Projection Spreadsheet Matrix
- 5-Step Lead Nurturing Email / Content Cadence
